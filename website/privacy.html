Privacy

Sidenote and your data.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Local-first by default

Sidenote stores your notebooks, notes, tags, attachments, versions, and settings in your browser's IndexedDB. With no server configured, the app makes no network request of its own and works with no network at all.

Optional sync

The public demo at sidenoteapp.vercel.app is local-first: it is not connected to a shared sync service, so notes stay in your browser. If you self-host Sidenote with your own Supabase instance, signing in sends your notes and their metadata to that instance so they can be restored on another device. The operator of that instance is whoever runs the deployment.

Share links

A share link you create is public to anyone who has the URL, optionally protected by a password and an expiry date. The shared note is served from the configured Supabase instance. Do not share a note containing information you do not want to leave your control.

No third parties

Sidenote has no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, error-reporting service, or AI provider. It does not load fonts or scripts from a CDN. The only outward connections are:

  • the Supabase instance configured for sync and share links, if any;
  • the origin serving the app's own assets and service worker.

Your controls

You can export a ZIP backup at any time from Settings → Backup, remove sample data, sign out, or delete all local data. If you use sync, the operator of that Supabase instance controls the server-side copy; there is no hidden copy anywhere else.

Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at github.com/awbuilds/sidenote. For security reports, use the private advisory flow in SECURITY.md.
